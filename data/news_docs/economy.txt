India's economy recorded steady growth during the latest financial quarter, driven by increased manufacturing output, higher consumer spending, and continued investment in infrastructure projects. Government officials reported improvements in industrial production and exports, while inflation remained within the expected range.

Economists noted that sectors such as renewable energy, information technology, and construction contributed significantly to economic performance. Small and medium-sized enterprises also showed signs of recovery due to improved access to digital services and government support initiatives.

Experts believe that maintaining stable inflation, encouraging private investment, and expanding digital infrastructure will be important for sustaining long-term economic growth. Analysts also emphasized the need for continued investment in education and skill development to strengthen the country's workforce and improve productivity.
