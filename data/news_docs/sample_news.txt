India successfully launched its latest Earth observation satellite from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre. The mission aims to improve weather forecasting, disaster management, and environmental monitoring across the country.

According to ISRO officials, the satellite was placed into its intended orbit successfully, and all onboard systems are functioning normally. Scientists stated that the satellite will collect high-resolution images of the Earth's surface and provide valuable data for agriculture, urban planning, and climate research.

Experts believe the mission strengthens India's position in space technology and supports scientific research by providing accurate and timely environmental data. Government agencies and research institutions are expected to use the information collected by the satellite to improve decision-making in areas such as flood prediction, forest monitoring, and natural resource management.
